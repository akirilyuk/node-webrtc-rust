# Short-rejoin bench (chunk 7c, decision dd)

`short_rejoin_bench.rs` asks whether the audio of a key's `short` checks (less VAD speech than
`min_check_speech_ms`, never scored today) can tell a bystander from the caller well enough to
split at the VAD gap when the caller rejoins that key (eu-load 43698709106, iteration 20). Method
and corpora are in the file header. No audio or embeddings are committed.

```text
cargo run --release -p node-webrtc-rust-vendor-sherpa-onnx --example short_rejoin_bench -- \
  --voices-root <.models> --speaker-dir <.models/speaker> --bed <tocumen48.f32> \
  --cache <dir> --librispeech <LibriSpeech dir> [--bed-db -12] [--out <file.md>]
```

## Reading the result

- **Bar (2), paused caller never splits:** fails at the default `CONFIDENT_OTHER_MAX_SCORE` 0.20.
  LibriSpeech callers score below 0.20 on their own short audio at every voiced length
  (1 or 2 per 2448 samples from 800 ms up, 32 at 400 ms). Lowest caller scores from 800 ms:
  0.137, 0.172, 0.193, 0.180 (800 / 1000 / 1200 / 1500 ms).
- The bar holds at `< 0.10` from 800 ms voiced (0 / 2448 at each length) and at `< 0.15` from
  1000 ms. The margin to the lowest caller score is 0.04 (0.10 bound) and 0.02 (0.15 bound).
- **Bar (1), bystander splits:** at `< 0.10` from 800 ms, 25-35 % of Piper and 58-68 % of
  LibriSpeech bystander clips split. For the incident pair (caller amy-low, bystander
  hfc_female-medium), 0-17 % split at 800-1500 ms. At 0.20 the same pair splits 25-83 %.
- Embedding a short clip takes 12 ms p50 / 27 ms p99 on one thread, well inside the 500 ms verify
  budget.
