The children built a tall snowman in the garden and gave him a carrot nose and a woolly scarf.
On rainy afternoons my grandmother baked apple pies and sang old songs while the kitchen windows steamed.
The orchestra tuned their instruments while the audience slowly found their seats beneath the grand painted ceiling.
Two hikers followed the rocky trail beside the cliffs and stopped to watch the waves crash far below.
The bakery on the corner sells warm croissants, cinnamon rolls and crusty loaves to sleepy students at dawn.

The painter mixed bright colors on her palette and sketched the lighthouse standing proudly against a stormy sky.
Our neighbors planted roses, lavender and tall sunflowers beside the wooden fence between their yard and ours.
